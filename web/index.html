How it works

Three steps, a few minutes, nothing to install.

  1. 1

    Upload bill + EOB

    Your itemized bill and the matching Explanation of Benefits. Digital PDFs from your portals work best; photos and scans work too.

  2. 2

    Check we're sending the right pages

    You see the exact pages that will be sent, laid out large, before anything leaves your browser. Check they're the right documents, the right way up, and that nothing is cut off — if a page is unreadable here, the audit will be wrong too. The pages are then sent to Google's Gemini models on Vertex AI to be read.

  3. 3

    Read your findings

    Duplicate charges, billing above the EOB allowed amount, cost-share math, and — if your plan is on file — charges that contradict what it promised, each quoting the line it came from. Audit more than one bill and it also catches the same visit charged on two different statements, which no single bill can show you. Or a clean result: your bill checks out.
