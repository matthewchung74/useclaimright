## The reason to do it now, before making anything

**YouTube Studio → Research.** It is the only free source of real YouTube search
volume, and it is gated behind having a channel. Everything in `docs/CONTENT.md`
about demand is inferred from Trends and autocomplete; Research replaces the
inference with YouTube's own numbers.

First three things to type into it, in order:

- `medical bill`
- `dispute medical bill`
- `explanation of benefits`

**Run on 2026-09-14. Every relevant term came back Low**, and YouTube does not
recognise "explanation of benefits" as an insurance topic at all. Full figures in
`docs/CONTENT.md` — the written-page conclusion stands.

## Name and handle

**Recommended: `UseClaimRight`, handle `@useclaimright`.**

Matches the site and the GitHub org, and a person who liked a Short can find the
tool without a search. The counter-argument is real — a brand channel giving
consumer advice can read as an advert, and a topic name like "Check Your Bill"
would not. But splitting the name from the product means building two things at
once, and there is only one of you.

If `@useclaimright` is taken, `@useclaimright_` is better than a different word.

## What not to do yet

- **No banner, no trailer, no "welcome to my channel".** Nothing is watched
  before there is something to watch.
- **No upload schedule commitment.** The plan is explicitly slow.
- **Do not link it from the site yet.** An empty channel linked from a live
  product looks abandoned; an empty channel nobody knows about looks like
  nothing.
